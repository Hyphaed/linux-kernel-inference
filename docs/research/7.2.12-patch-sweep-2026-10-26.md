## Series on 7.2.12

`30-validate-series.sh` applied all 26 patches with a sequential `git am --3way` on v7.2.12 and compiled the 32
objects they touch: "series validated on v7.2.12" (**measured**). One file drifted under a patch,
`kernel/sched/sched.h`: context movement, still applies. v7.2.11..v7.2.12 is 13 commits (**read**): one
`sched_ext` hotplug fix, KVM memslots, ksmbd, dm-integrity, mana, nfsd, nullfs, readdir, ASoC, ath9k revert.
Nothing touches a path this box runs except the `sched_ext` fix, which only bites a loaded sched_ext scheduler
plus CPU hotplug (`scx_bpfland` is loaded, nothing here hot-unplugs). The 7.2.11 "wait for stable" item is closed.
