# 7.2.15: performance commits from 7.3 that can be brought back safely (2026-11-04)

The question was "everything that improves performance of 7.2.15 that can be safely brought from 7.3". This is the answer
as far as it can be given without booting: what 7.3 changed, what applies, what compiles, what I left out and why.
Labels: **measured** (ran here), **read** (read the code or message), **inferred**, **not checked**.

## How the list was built

1. `scripts/bump/22-perf-candidates.py --target v7.2.15 --donor v7.3` lists the 19,217 development commits in 7.3, drops the
   5,828 that stable already took (matched by the `commit <sha> upstream` line in each backport), keeps the 3,006 that touch a
   subsystem this box runs, and flags 474 that claim a performance effect (116 of them quote a number).
2. `22b-perf-apply-test.py` tries each of those 474 with `git cherry-pick` on 7.2.15 + the default series: **302 apply
   cleanly, 171 conflict, 1 is already there** (**measured**).
3. I read the 302 and kept the ones that are small, reviewed, and not refactors, tests, documentation, or hardware this
   machine lacks (ARM, AMD GPU, Hyper-V, mlx5, NFS...). That's judgment, not a score: the claim is only as good as the
   commit message, and none of it has been measured here.
4. The picks were stacked in dependency order and compiled together on 7.2.15 + default series: 44 picked, **5 dropped
   because they need other 7.3 code the compiler found** (below), **39 left, 23 touched objects compile, no errors**. The official
   stage 30 then validated default + experimental on v7.2.15 (49 objects). Not linked or booted yet.

## Before the list: something that changes how much any of it is worth

`sched_ext` is enabled on this machine: `hyphaed-scx.service` runs `scx_bpfland` with `switch_all=1`, so every normal task
is scheduled by the BPF scheduler, not by the fair class. Consequences:

* Fair-class commits (the single-runqueue EEVDF work, Intel's hybrid-core balancing fixes, `cgroup_mode`) change nothing
  for your day-to-day load while that service runs. They matter at boot before the service starts, and if it stops.
* The scheduler work that could matter to you is whether `scx_bpfland` still loads on the 7.3 `sched_ext` carried by
  the `sched73` variant. Not checked yet (planned VM test).
* Everything below is outside the scheduler, so it applies regardless.

The TLB rework is the one with a real latency argument: remote TLB flushes can stall the issuing CPU "for tens of
milliseconds" with preemption off, and 7.3 stops pinning the CPU for that wait (**read**, commit message; not measured).
It needed a prerequisite (`33c179b5`, found by the first conflict). The five `maple_tree` edits claim 0.6 to 3.5 %
on a micro-benchmark and have no review tags, which is why they sit in B.

## Considered and not taken

* **171 commits that conflict.** Most need an earlier 7.3 series (the swap_ops rework, `vma_flags_t`, the cpuset attach
  rework). Looked at individually: `3c5194eb` (zram: single-folio writes) fixes a regression from a swap change 7.2 doesn't
  have (**inferred**: it conflicts for that reason), `7b9f4e5f` (lru_lock contention, 176 lines), `9a560af1` (task-local
  IPI mask, 104 lines), `07961ebb` (ext4 unaligned DIO, 108 lines), `572af687` (AES mode override fix) and the zsmalloc
  `zs_free` lock removal are too large or too entangled to call safe without reading them in full.
* **Fair-class and `sched_ext` commits**: covered by the `sched73` variant, not repeated here.
* **KSM** (`ksm: optimize rmap_walk_ksm...`, `ksm: add linear_page_index...`): `ksm run=1` is on here, but these are a
  two-patch rmap series I judged from the titles only (**not read**); left for a round that has a number to chase.
* **Maple tree `LOCKDEP` check, kcfi, devmem, zcrx, hid ids, battery, ARM**: not this machine.

## Interaction with the other series

* **`sched73` variant:** the list stacks on top of it with one conflict: `b0bc1c75` (PSI irqtime shortcut), because the
  variant's 7.3 `psi.c` already contains it (**measured**: 38 of 39 apply). Comment that one patch out when building the variant.
* **Neri backports (0031-0035):** independent files, no conflict.

## Not checked

Booting; whether any of it is measurably faster on this 14900KF; link and modules (the real `make bump` does that);
interaction with `scx_bpfland` at runtime; the claimed numbers in the commit messages are the authors', not mine. Promote
nothing to the default series until the two-run A/B (`hyphaed/round.py`) shows a gain.
